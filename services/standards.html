Service Area

International Standards & Compliance

Standards influence how technology is built, how AI is governed, and how systems work together across borders. We take part in several standards bodies and help you understand which standards matter for you, now and in the coming years.

Bodies We Engage With
ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC42
The main international subcommittee for AI standards, covering AI systems, trustworthiness, data quality and governance
W3C
World Wide Web Consortium: web standards, accessibility and semantic technologies
CEN/CLC
The European standardisation committees (CEN and CENELEC): European standards, including those that support EU regulation
MLCommons
AI benchmarking, safety evaluations, and performance standards for machine learning systems
Linux Foundation
Open-source governance, security, and compliance across the software supply chain
How We Can Help

SC42 Standards Navigation

Help finding your way through the work of ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC42: which published standards, technical reports and drafts are relevant to your AI systems.

Standards Development Updates

Regular updates on SC42 drafts, new work items and upcoming publications, so that new requirements do not take you by surprise.

Independent Perspective

An informed, independent view on how relevant standards may apply to your AI systems or processes, to support your own decision-making. This is a perspective, not an assessment or certification.

Standards Landscape Briefing

An overview of the wider set of standards: which ones apply to your product or process, and how they relate to regulation.

AI Act Awareness

A general overview of what the EU AI Act covers, its current timeline, and how it relates to international standards, so you can have better-informed conversations with your legal and compliance advisers.

Standards Participation Strategy

Advice on whether and how to engage with standards bodies to shape frameworks that affect your industry.

Policy & Standards Interpretation

Plain-language explanations of complex technical standards documents and their practical implications for your team.
