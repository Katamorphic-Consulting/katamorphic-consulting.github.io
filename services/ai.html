AI & Technology Advisory

AI changes quickly, and it is not always clear which developments matter to you. We explain recent research and industry developments in plain terms, whether you are new to AI or already using it.

What We Cover

AI Usage & Adoption Advice

Practical advice on choosing and using AI tools and systems, including how teams and organisations can use them responsibly.

Data Analysis, Science & Visualisation

From exploratory analysis and statistical modelling to charts that communicate results clearly, based on hands-on experience in research and industry.

Independent Perspective

An informed, independent view on AI systems, methodologies, datasets, or strategies: a second opinion free from vendor or implementation bias, to support your own decisions.

Trends & Horizon Scanning

Keep up with new models and frameworks, from large language models to multimodal systems and AI agents.

Concept Explanation & Education

Clear explanations of AI concepts for technical and non-technical audiences, from embeddings and fine-tuning to governance and explainability.

Open-Source Ecosystem

Advice on open-source tools, informed by our involvement with communities such as the Linux Foundation, OSSEU and FOSDEM.

Implementation Guidance

Practical advice on everything from data pipelines to model deployment, based on software engineering and data science experience.

AI Risk & Ethics

The social and regulatory side of AI, explained with reference to our work with international standards bodies.
