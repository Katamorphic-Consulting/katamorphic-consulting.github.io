We Empower Individuals and Companies with Bespoke Solutions

Make sense of international and national standards, starting with a conversation.

Katamorphic takes its name from “kata”, the Japanese idea of practised form and pattern, combined with transformation. In technical terminology, katamorphic describes processes that break rock down through chemical or mechanical forces. We do something similar with complex questions, breaking them down until they are clear. You talk to someone who takes part in the international committees where AI standards are written, and come away knowing what is changing, what it might mean for you, and which questions to take to your lawyers, auditors or vendors.

Insight QuickCall

On request

A focused conversation on a specific question: where do we stand, and what should we be asking?

Ideal for: Getting oriented quickly.

Examples:

  • AI & data: a focused question, for example "Which LLM framework is right for our use case?" or "How do I visualise this dataset?"
  • Standards: a specific SC42 question, for example "Which standards are relevant to our AI system?"
  • Cross-cultural: quick advice on a specific situation, for example "How do I approach this negotiation with a Chinese partner?"
  • Education: a focused question about planning a workshop, talk, or team learning session

Extended Conversations

On request

A few conversations to explore a broader topic in depth, one-to-one or with your team.

Ideal for: Building a shared understanding of a complex or fast-moving topic.

Examples:

  • AI & data: exploring an approach, weighing options, or building internal understanding
  • Standards: understanding how the AI Act and relevant standards relate to your work, and what to ask your advisers
  • Cross-cultural: market entry preparation, a major international project, or building team cross-cultural competence
  • Education: developing a workshop or structured coaching over several sessions

Fractional Advisor

On request

A standing sounding board: regular conversations to talk through developments as they happen, at a pace that suits you.

Ideal for: Long-term and adaptable support.

Examples:

  • AI & data: ongoing fractional advisor role as you implement and evolve your initiatives
  • Standards: regular conversations on how standards and regulation are developing
  • Cross-cultural: ongoing trusted advisor across an extended international engagement
  • Education: ongoing mentoring or long-term team capability building

About Katamorphic

Katamorphic Consulting helps people and organisations understand where technology, standards and international work meet, and what that means for their own decisions.

Our background includes PhDs in AI, data science, software engineering and cybersecurity, and Masters degrees in Applied Mathematics and Engineering, with work experience in the UK, France, China, the Netherlands and the US.

We are actively involved with ISO, W3C, CEN/CLC and the Linux Foundation. We keep up with research and industry through academic conferences such as ICLR and ACL, industry events such as MWC and IBC, and open-source communities such as OSSEU and FOSDEM.

If you have a question in one of these areas, we would be glad to hear from you.
