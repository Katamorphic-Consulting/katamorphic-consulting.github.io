Insight QuickCall

On request

A focused conversation on a specific question: where do we stand, and what should we be asking?

Ideal for: Getting oriented quickly.

Examples:

  • AI & data: a focused question — e.g. "Which LLM framework is right for our use case?" or "How do I visualise this dataset?"
  • Standards: a specific SC42 question — e.g. "Which standards are relevant to our AI system?"
  • Cross-cultural: quick advice on a specific situation — e.g. "How do I approach this negotiation with a Chinese partner?"
  • Education: a focused question about planning a workshop, talk, or team learning session

Extended Conversations

On request

A few conversations to explore a broader topic in depth, one-to-one or with your team.

Ideal for: Building a shared understanding of a complex or fast-moving topic.

Examples:

  • AI & data: exploring an approach, weighing options, or building internal understanding
  • Standards: understanding how the AI Act and relevant standards relate to your work, and what to ask your advisers
  • Cross-cultural: market entry preparation, a major international project, or building team cross-cultural competence
  • Education: developing a workshop or structured coaching over several sessions

Fractional Advisor

About Katamorphic

At Katamorphic Consulting, our mission is to facilitate a productive exchange of information and uncover hidden opportunities in the modern landscape where technology and global engagement converge.

Our ability to support your processes is built on a foundation of multi-disciplinary expertise, including PhDs in AI, data science, software engineering, and cybersecurity, and Masters in Applied Mathematics and Engineering, with experiences across the UK, France, China, the Netherlands, and the US.

We have active engagement with key organisations such as ISO, W3C, CEN/CLC, and the Linux Foundation to ensure the robustness and compliance, built for the long term. Our perspective is continuously sharpened at the forefront of development, from academic conferences (e.g. ICLR, ACL) to industry-defining summits (e.g. MWC, IBC) and engaging with the core of the open-source community (e.g. OSSEU, FOSDEM).

Our purpose is to help you translate complexity into clarity. Reach out to discover what awaits.
